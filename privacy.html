Privacy Policy

File Compressor · Effective October 21, 2026

In short: File Compressor does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your videos are processed on your iPhone and never uploaded anywhere by the app.

What the app works with

The app processes the videos you choose. Compression and GIF conversion happen entirely on your device. The results are saved to the app's temporary storage on your device until you share them, save them, or iOS clears that storage. The app does not send your videos, or anything derived from them, to any server.

Photos access

  • Choosing videos: the app uses Apple's system photo picker. It only receives the videos you select and does not need access to your library.
  • Saving results: if you tap Save, the app asks for permission to add items to your photo library. This permission only lets the app add new items; it cannot read your library.
  • Deleting originals (optional): if you tap Delete Original, the app asks for full Photos access at that moment, only to find the video you converted. It asks you to confirm, and iOS then shows its own delete prompt. Nothing is deleted unless you confirm. Deleted videos go to Recently Deleted.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts or sign-in.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • It does not make network requests.
  • It does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers, or usage data.

Sharing

When you use the iOS share sheet, you decide where a file goes (for example Messages, Mail, or another app). That destination's own privacy policy applies to what happens next.

Apple

If you downloaded the app from the App Store or TestFlight, Apple may collect diagnostics or analytics according to your device settings and Apple's privacy policy. If you choose to share crash data with developers, it is delivered through Apple, not through the app.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.

Contact
